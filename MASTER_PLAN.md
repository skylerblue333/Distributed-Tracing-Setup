# SKYCOIN4444 Standalone Product Master Plan

Product #11: **Sky Observe**.

Sky Observe is the independently deployable observability foundation for traces, metrics and structured logs across SKYCOIN4444 services. It will expose OpenTelemetry-compatible instrumentation/configuration, trace propagation, health/readiness, example dashboards/exporters, secure configuration guidance, integration tests and CI evidence.

Future integration occurs through versioned telemetry contracts and SDK adapters. No managed monitoring service, SLA, retention guarantee or multi-region collector claim is made without deployment evidence.
